Stripe activation guide
Follow these steps to start accepting real payments
2 of 6 done
✓
Create a Stripe account
Go to stripe.com and create a free account using your business email. Takes 2 minutes.
✓
Add business details
Enter your business name (Dr. Bike Sydney), ABN, and bank account for payouts. Required by Australian law.
3
Get your Stripe API keys
In Stripe Dashboard → Developers → API keys. Copy your "Publishable key" and "Secret key". You'll need both.
4
Add keys to Dr. Bike app
Share your Stripe keys with Claude and I'll integrate real payment processing into the Dr. Bike app automatically — no code needed on your end.
STRIPE_PUBLIC_KEY=pk_live_...
STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_live_...
5
Create subscription products
I'll create the 3 membership plans (Basic $57, Standard $97, VIP $147) directly in your Stripe account with monthly and annual billing options.
6
Go live
Switch from test mode to live mode in Stripe. The Dr. Bike app starts accepting real card payments, Apple Pay and subscription billing automatically.
