Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2026  ·  Last updated: 14 June 2026

1. Our Commitment to Privacy

Dr. Bike Sydney ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

By using our website or services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

We only collect information necessary to provide and improve our services. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

2. Who We Are

Dr. Bike Sydney is a mobile bicycle repair and maintenance business operating in Sydney, NSW, Australia.

3. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

Information you provide directly:

  • Name - to identify you and address communications
  • Email address - for booking confirmations, receipts and service updates
  • Phone number - for booking reminders, scheduling and mechanic coordination
  • Service address - to dispatch our mechanic to your location
  • Bicycle details (brand, model, type) - to prepare appropriate tools and parts
  • Service history - records of work performed on your bicycle
  • Communication content - messages sent through our chat or email

Payment information:

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not collect, store or process your card number, CVV or full payment credentials. Stripe handles all payment data in compliance with PCI DSS standards. We receive only transaction confirmation details (amount, status, last 4 digits of card).

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • IP address (anonymised for analytics)
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referring URL
  • Device type (mobile, desktop)

This information is collected via Google Analytics and our hosting infrastructure (Vercel).

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process and confirm your bookings
  • Dispatch and coordinate our mechanics
  • Send booking reminders, service updates and receipts via email and SMS
  • Process payments through Stripe
  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Maintain a record of services performed on your bicycle
  • Improve our services and website based on usage patterns
  • Send marketing communications (only with your consent - you may unsubscribe at any time)
  • Comply with our legal obligations

We will not use your information for purposes that are incompatible with the original purpose of collection, except where required by law or with your consent.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We share your information only with the service providers necessary to operate our business. Each is bound by their own privacy policies and applicable law.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Maintain your session and booking progress
  • Analyse website traffic (Google Analytics)
  • Remember your preferences

Google Analytics is configured with IP anonymisation. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. This may affect some functionality of our website.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet our legal obligations:

  • Booking and service records - 7 years (required for tax and financial compliance under Australian law)
  • Account information - for the duration of your account plus 2 years after last activity
  • Marketing consent records - until you withdraw consent
  • Website logs - 90 days (Vercel default)

When data is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification and disclosure. These steps include:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit
  • Database access controls and row-level security via Supabase
  • Payment data handled exclusively by Stripe (PCI DSS compliant)
  • Access limited to authorised personnel only

No data transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you have reason to believe your interaction with us is no longer secure, please contact us immediately at contact@drbikesydney.com.au.

9. Notifiable Data Breaches

We comply with the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme under Part IIIC of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If we become aware of an eligible data breach that is likely to result in serious harm to individuals whose information is involved, we will:

  • Notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as soon as practicable
  • Notify affected individuals directly where required
  • Take immediate steps to contain and remediate the breach

10. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate, out-of-date or incomplete personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to our legal retention obligations)
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Complain about a breach of the APPs

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@drbikesydney.com.au. We will respond within 30 days. There is no fee for making a request, though we may charge a reasonable fee for providing access if the request involves substantial effort.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Marketing Communications

We may send you promotional emails or SMS messages about our services only if you have opted in. Each marketing communication includes an unsubscribe option. You can also opt out at any time by emailing contact@drbikesydney.com.au with "Unsubscribe" in the subject line.

We comply with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) for all electronic marketing.

13. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page with the effective date noted at the top. We will notify you of material changes via email if you have an account with us.

15. Complaints
