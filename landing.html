Your protection

You're covered. Every visit.

We carry full Public Liability Insurance so you can book with complete peace of mind.

Public Liability — $2M
We hold a $2M Public Liability Insurance policy. If anything goes wrong at your property during our visit — damage or injury — you're covered.
Verified mechanics
All Dr. Bike mechanics are background-checked and trained. You'll always know who's coming — name, photo and rating shown before arrival.
30-day guarantee
Not happy with the work? We'll come back and fix it for free within 30 days. No questions asked.
What's covered
  • Property damage during service
  • Injury to third parties at your location
  • Workmanship errors (30-day fix guarantee)
  • Parts installed by our mechanics
Not covered
  • Pre-existing damage to your bike
  • Parts supplied by the client
  • Bike theft (we recommend a separate policy)
  • Wear & tear from normal use
