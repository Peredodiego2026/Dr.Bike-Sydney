## Class 37 word marks of this shape keep failing

Both prior attempts lapsed as **"Not accepted"**:
- N.1638847 "bike dr" (Class 37, 41) - Dismantle Inc
- N.1839606 "BikeDr." (Class 37) - Dismantle

That is the pattern behind the adverse report on The Bike Doctor too: plain
words of the "bike doctor" shape read as descriptive in Class 37. **This is why
the strategy is the figurative LOGO + composite mark ("Dr. Bike" + slogan), not
the bare words** - the word route has failed for everyone who tried it.

## Our own exposure

- An active **"DR BIKE" business name** exists in **VIC 3083** (ABN
